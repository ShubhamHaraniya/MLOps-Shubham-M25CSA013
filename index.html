Assignment 4

Optimizing Transformer Translation

Built a custom Encoder-Decoder Transformer for English→Hindi translation and optimised it using Ray Tune (Optuna + ASHA) over 7 hyperparameters — beating the baseline BLEU in 70% fewer epochs.

52.37% Tuned BLEU
44.62% Baseline BLEU
5.4× Speedup
30 Epochs (vs 100)
3.7 min Train Time
  • Architecture: Custom Transformer (dmodel=512, 46.8M params baseline → 23.7M tuned) trained on 3,956 English–Hindi pairs.
  • Search: Ray Tune + Optuna searched 7 hyperparameters (lr, batch size, heads, d_ff, dropout, layers, warmup) over 10 trials with ASHA early stopping.
  • Best Config: lr=3.20e-4, batch=32, heads=4, d_ff=1024, dropout=0.254, layers=4 — leaner and better regularised than baseline.
  • Result: Tuned model beat baseline BLEU (52.37% vs 44.62%) using only 30 epochs in 3.7 min vs 19.7 min — a 5.4× speedup and 70% epoch reduction.
