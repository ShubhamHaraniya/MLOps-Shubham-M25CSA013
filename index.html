ResNet & SVM Analysis

Explored Hyperparameter Tuning for ResNet-18/50 and SVMs on MNIST/FashionMNIST.


  • Best Model: ResNet-18 (Adam, LR 0.001) achieved ~99.36% accuracy on MNIST.
  • Efficiency: ResNet-18 matched ResNet-50 performance but with significantly faster training times.

HuggingFace Model Training & Docker Deployment

End-to-end MLOps pipeline: fine-tuned DistilBERT on Goodreads book reviews for 8-genre classification with Docker containerization.


  • Model: distilbert-base-uncased (66M params) — 61.88% accuracy on 8 genres.
  • Best Genres: Comics & Graphic (F1: 0.81), Poetry (F1: 0.77) due to distinctive vocabulary.
  • Pipeline: Train → Evaluate → Push to HuggingFace Hub → Re-evaluate from Hub (0.0000 diff).
  • Deployment: GPU-enabled Docker images for both development and production evaluation.
